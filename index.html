Ask a question

Is there plastic in Alexandria’s sea and fish?

Yes. Ten peer-reviewed studies found microplastics in the city’s seawater, beach sand and commercial fish. Search the sourced answers below.

The short version

  • 91.8%of commercial fish samples held microplastics (El-Sayed et al., 2022)
  • 457.4 per m³in Alexandria’s surface water (Abdel Ghani et al., 2022)
  • Not proven harmfulto people yet; research on human exposure is still needed
